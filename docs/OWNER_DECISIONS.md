# Owner Business & Operational Decisions Questionnaire

**Client:** Godfrey Mugisha (Owner, Kembabazi Unisex Salon, Mbarara)  
**Date:** Live Demo Session

---

### 1. Pricing & Discount Limits
- **Current Prototype Setting:** Receptionist (Mary) can discount up to **UGX 5,000** on her own authority; any discount higher requires Joan's (Manager) in-app PIN approval.
- **Questions for Godfrey:**
  1. Is UGX 5,000 the appropriate threshold for receptionist discretion, or should it be lower/higher?
  2. Should discounts be flat amounts (UGX) or percentage-based (e.g. 5%, 10%)?
  3. Does the owner want an SMS or push notification whenever a manager approves a discount exceeding UGX 20,000?

---

### 2. Cash Float & Physical Cash Variance Tolerance
- **Current Prototype Setting:** Opening cash float is UGX 50,000; Day Close prompts for physical cash count.
- **Questions for Godfrey:**
  1. What is the standard opening cash float kept in the drawer?
  2. What is the allowable discrepancy before the manager must call you directly (e.g. UGX 5,000 or any amount)?
  3. Who deposits physical cash into the salon bank account, and at what cadence (daily, every 2 days, weekly)?

---

### 3. Mobile Money Reconciliation Cadence
- **Current Prototype Setting:** Daily CSV statement import from MTN MoMo / Airtel Money portal.
- **Questions for Godfrey:**
  1. Does Joan (Manager) have direct view-only access to download the daily MoMo merchant statement, or does the finance accountant send it?
  2. How do you currently handle customers who pay with MoMo and include the withdrawal fee or a tip? (Currently mapped as 'MoMo Tip' or 'Excess payment').

---

### 4. Staff Shifts & Multi-Step Service Handoffs
- **Current Prototype Setting:** Hair washing and hair styling are separate steps in the ticket traveler, allowing different stylists to handle parts of one visit.
- **Questions for Godfrey:**
  1. How are tips or performance bonuses calculated when two stylists work on one client (e.g. Grace washes, Sarah braids)?
  2. What is the salon's policy when a stylist is more than 30 minutes late without notification?
